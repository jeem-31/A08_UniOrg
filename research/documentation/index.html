Platform scope

UNIORG is a configurable Laravel web platform for OSAS and student organizations. Its manuscript describes organization workspaces, accreditation, events and approvals, payment tracking, elections, academic resources, communication, role-based access and OCR/NLP document processing. The research covers LSPU Santa Cruz Campus.
